    Storyboard

    Most Hexicans hold HEX on both chains, because PulseChain copied every Ethereum balance, stakes included, when it launched. So the video opens there and turns on its second line: your name should be on both too. After that, each scene answers one question, and everything the voice says is also on screen, since most people watch X with the sound off.

    The script as recorded. If you re-record a line, paste it from here as it is: the odd spellings make the voice say each ticker the way Hexicans do.

    2. Your name should too.

    4. Buy it once on PulseChain, and it's copied to Ethereum automatically.

    Voice

    Warm, confident and friendly: someone who holds HEX explaining it to a friend. Not a trailer voice, and not hype. Audition three voices on the first two lines; they carry the whole video.

    Settings

    On Multilingual v2: stability 45–55%, similarity 75%, style 15–25%, speaker boost on, speed 1.0. If the take runs past 22 seconds, raise the speed to 1.05–1.1 rather than cutting words.

    Delivery

    A small pause after “two chains.” so “Your name should too.” lands as the turn. A little urgency on “now”, and a short beat before the address. A smile on the last line.

    Look and motion

    Three curves do all the work. Each dot below moves on the curve it's named after; the After Effects note gives the same feel with Easy Ease.

    • No hard cuts. Something from every shot carries into the next: cards become logos, the name pill drops into the send screen, becomes a name card, then the checkout. At the end, the search bar becomes the button.
    • Blur is depth. Things arrive out of a 12–18 px blur and leave into one, which hides the start of every move.
    • One move at a time. While one thing moves, everything else only floats.
    • Light from below. A slow amber-to-magenta glow drifts along the bottom of the frame for the whole video, brightening at the logo moments.
    • Grain. 3% film grain over everything, so X's compression doesn't band the gradients.
